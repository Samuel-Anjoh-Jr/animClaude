# Story formula

All three reference films use the same skeleton. Timings are for a ~2:30 film; scale them.

| # | Beat | Share | What happens | Typical devices |
|---|------|-------|--------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Loop** | 0–10% | "Every day started the same." The hero fails at the anchor moment (alarm, payday, phone). | black caption card, bedroom, clock, small DAY label |
| 2 | **Enemy appears** | 10–30% | Shadow fades in beside the hero and whispers. Days jump (1 → 25 → 47 → 112 → 203) to show time wasted. | couch/desk/bed scenes, red typewriter whispers |
| 3 | **Identity attack** | 30–35% | Mirror scene: the reflection IS the shadow. A huge red whisper attacks identity ("you're lazy"). Mirror cracks. | mirror, big whisper, boom SFX |
| 4 | **Rock bottom** | 35–40% | Hero curled in a spotlight. "He believed it." Silence. | spotlight set, music silent |
| 5 | **Reframe / turn** | 40–45% | Narrator reframes ("Lazy isn't who you are. It's what you repeat."). Anchor returns in close-up (4:59 → 5:00), hero hits it, big "DAY 1." | clock_closeup + alarm, flash, headband appears |
| 6 | **The climb** | 45–85% | Training montage with day jumps. Enemy keeps whispering, louder ("quit.", "nobody cares.") — hero keeps going. One relapse/fall beat, then gets up. Stairs: the enemy blocks the way and the hero pushes through. | city run, push-ups, punching bag, stairs, meter rising |
| 7 | **Summit + bookend + title** | 85–100% | Sunset summit with the thesis line, back at the opening anchor but changed (enemy tiny in the corner), then the title card: WORD / sub-line / red punch line. | hill + sparks, dawn bedroom, title |

## Whisper ladder (escalate)

1. Comfort: "just 5 more minutes…", "you deserve it.", "tomorrow."
2. Doubt: "you're tired.", "why even try?", "save next month."
3. Identity: "you're lazy.", "you're bad with money.", "this is who you are."
4. Isolation: "nobody cares.", "nobody is watching."
5. Final: "quit.", "you can't."

## Variant structures from the references

- **Meter film** (*Motivation Will Fail You*): put a `meter` on screen. It drains each attempt and the day counter resets ("DAY 10" crossed out → "DAY 1"). The enemy sells a refill ("take it, you need it" +100%) that fades. The turn is "he stopped waiting for it". Ends on a graph: spikes vs staircase.
- **Generational film** (*Lust vs Love*): two time periods (child → adult, DAY 1,826). An elder's deathbed confession ("don't wait like I did") is the trigger; the hero breaks the chain by having the talk with his own child at the same age he was. Uses speech bubbles, warmer daylight palettes, a single saturated colour object (yellow umbrella) for the moment of protection. Ending sting for series retention.
