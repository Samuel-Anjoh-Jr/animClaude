# Style bible

## Frame
- 16:9, mostly empty. One floor line at ~78% height; the hero is small in a big dark space (loneliness). Close-ups are rare and save for emotion peaks (clock, face).
- Film grain + soft vignette always on. Every scene fades in/out from black (0.3–0.4 s); hard cuts only on hits.

## Characters
- **Hero:** pictogram figure in cream (#ECE5D4) with dark ink outline: separate round head, rounded-shoulder torso, thick round-ended limbs, gap between legs. Face = eyes, brows, mouth only. Emotion through brows + mouth only. Red headband = the "decided" version (appears from the turn onwards).
- **Enemy:** solid near-black silhouette, slightly taller than the hero, red outer glow that pulses, curved red eye slits. Stands *behind* the hero or in the mirror. Shrinks over the film.
- Family films add simple coloured clothing and hair; still faceless-simple.

## Colour script
| Act | Palette | Notes |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Loop / enemy | `night`, `cold` | grey on charcoal, moonlit window |
| Rock bottom | `black` + spotlight | only light in the film is on the hero |
| Turn | `night` with flash | red clock digits are the only colour |
| Climb | `night` → `warm` → `sunset` | streetlights, amber glow |
| End | `dawn`, `sunset` | warm sky in the same window as the opening |
Red is reserved for the enemy, the clock, the headband and the punch line.

## Type
- Captions: small, white, centred, top third, ≤12 words, on screen 2–4 s.
- Whispers: lowercase, red, typewriter reveal, near the enemy's head. Identity attacks go huge (40–60 px).
- Day label: tiny top-left ("DAY 47"); the turn uses a big centred "DAY 1."
- Title: one heavy word + hand-drawn red underline squiggle.
- Reference films use a rounded comic-style face (Comic Neue-like); this skill ships Poppins (OFL). Drop any .ttf into `assets/fonts/` named `Poppins-Bold.ttf` to swap.

## Pacing
- Opening and rock bottom: slow (4–6 s scenes, silence).
- Montage: fast (2.5–4 s, day jumps every scene).
- Title: 6–8 s, letters type in, underline draws, sub-lines fade in one by one.

## Sound
- Low minor drone for the fall; near-silence for rock bottom; alarm beeps on the turn; hopeful piano plucks for the climb; a sub boom on the mirror and the title.
- Narration: calm, low, close-mic, no music swell under the key line.
